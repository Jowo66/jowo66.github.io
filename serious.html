About me

I am a computer science student who learns by building. I would rather understand the rendering loop, the audio graph or the scheduler than import a library to hide it, and most of what I know came from making something work end to end and then fixing what was wrong with it.

Projects

Neo-Jowo OS (this portfolio, in its playful form)

JavaScript, Canvas 2D, Web Audio API. No frameworks, no build step, no external assets. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

  • A custom flat-shaded low-poly 3D renderer on a 2D canvas: boxes with taper, jointed limbs, painter's-algorithm sorting, fog and lighting.
  • A generative house-music synthesiser using the Web Audio API: seeded tracks across four styles (club, soul, latin and afro house), with drums, bass and chord voices built from oscillators and filtered noise.
  • Agent simulations: alley inhabitants with small state machines (walking, arguing, shopping, fleeing), a nightclub crowd with a moshpit and a dance-battle mode, and an isometric district map.
  • A window manager, a persistent playlist stored locally, and a day and night mode.
